04 / FAQ

Before you plan

What does break-even mean?

Break-even is the approximate number of orders needed for your contribution after product and payment costs to cover the event costs you entered. It does not include costs you leave out, taxes, or the value of your time unless you include them.

Are the sample numbers typical?

No. They are only starter values to demonstrate the tool. Replace them with your own booth fee, travel costs, product costs, average order value, and payment-processing rate.

Does MarketMath store my financial data?

The calculators run in your browser. The event planner can save a plan to local storage on the device you are using. Do not use a shared device if you do not want other people to see your saved plan.

Can I rely on the result as a guarantee?

No. The result is arithmetic based on your assumptions, not a forecast of customer demand or event performance. Actual sales may be higher or lower.
