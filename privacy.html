PRIVACY

Your numbers should stay yours.

This starter policy must be reviewed and updated to match the services you actually enable before publication.

Calculator inputs

The calculators run locally in your browser. MarketMath does not send the values you enter in the calculators to a MarketMath server. The event planner's “Save this plan” feature stores its values in local storage on your device until you clear them or your browser removes them.

Hosting and logs

Your hosting provider may collect standard server or access logs, such as IP address, browser information, and requested pages. Review the privacy terms of the host you choose and update this policy with any relevant details.

Advertising and analytics

No advertising or analytics service is configured in this starter version. If you add an advertising network, analytics tool, embedded content, or other third-party service, update this policy to explain what data it may collect and link to the provider's current policies. Cookie or consent requirements depend on your audience and the services you use.

Your choices

You can clear the saved event plan by clearing this site's local storage or your browser's site data. For privacy questions, add a real contact method to the contact page.

Site owner: Before publishing, replace this starter policy with a policy that accurately describes your actual setup and complies with applicable law.
