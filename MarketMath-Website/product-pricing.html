How the pricing calculation works

First, the calculator adds materials, packaging, labor, and other per-item overhead. It then accounts for the selling-fee percentage and solves for the price that would leave the target margin, based on the costs and fee rate you entered.

Suggested priceCost ÷ (1 − fee rate − target margin)

Example: if your entered cost is $10, fees are 3%, and your target margin is 40%, the estimate is $10 ÷ (1 − 0.03 − 0.40) = $17.54.

What this estimate leaves out

Include any costs that matter to your business, such as failed batches, shipping subsidies, refunds, taxes, or fixed monthly expenses. If a selling platform charges fixed fees per order, add an estimated per-item share under “Other overhead” or adjust the model to suit your situation. Confirm the final price against customer demand and comparable products.
