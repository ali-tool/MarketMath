ABOUT MARKETMATH

Clearer numbers for independent makers.

MarketMath is a small collection of free planning calculators for people who sell handmade products at craft fairs, pop-up markets, and community events.

Our purpose

Preparing for a market involves many small decisions: how much the event costs, what each product costs to make, how many orders cover the booth fee, and what price leaves room for profit. MarketMath helps organize those numbers in one place.

How the tools work

Calculations run in your browser using the inputs you provide. The results are estimates based on those assumptions. We aim to show the formulas clearly so you can check the arithmetic and adapt it to your own business.

What we do not promise

MarketMath does not predict demand, guarantee sales, provide tax or legal advice, or replace professional accounting. You are responsible for checking your own costs, platform fees, taxes, and business obligations.

Corrections and suggestions

If you find a calculation issue or have an idea for a useful tool, visit the contact page. Replace the example contact details there before publishing.
