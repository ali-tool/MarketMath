How to read the result

Contribution per order is the average order value minus product cost and the payment fee calculated as a percentage of revenue. Break-even orders equal fixed event costs divided by contribution per order, rounded up to the next whole order.

Break-even ordersCeiling(fixed event costs ÷ contribution per order)

If the contribution per order is zero or negative, this calculator cannot produce a useful break-even target. Review your price, product cost, and fee assumptions.

Before you book a booth

Consider whether the event audience matches your products, what comparable events have been like, and how much inventory and time you will commit. This calculator cannot estimate foot traffic, conversion rate, weather, or actual sales.
